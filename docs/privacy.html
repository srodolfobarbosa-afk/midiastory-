Privacy Policy & Data Safety Disclosure

Application: Midiastore (com.rdf112.midiastore) · Official Website: midiastory-df6a0.web.app

Effective & Last Updated: November 24, 2026

100% On-Device Scoped Storage Zero Photo / Video Uploads Zero MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Zero Advertising ID (AD_ID)

1. Overview & Core Privacy Commitment

Midiastore (com.rdf112.midiastore) is a pure on-device Android storage cleaner built with privacy by design. Your personal photos, videos, audio recordings, screenshots, and thumbnails stay strictly on your Android device and are never uploaded, backed up, or transmitted to external servers.

2. On-Device Media Access (Android MediaStore & Scoped Storage)

  • Granular Permissions Only: Midiastore accesses only media categories you authorize via standard Android permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED on Android 14+, or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 10–12).
  • Zero Broad File Access: Midiastore does not request or declare MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE ("All files access").
  • 100% Local Processing: Streaming SHA-256 exact-duplicate hashing, large video detection (>10 MB), old media & screenshot filtering (+30 days), and local blur analysis execute entirely inside your phone's processor and memory.
  • No Automatic Deletion: Midiastore never deletes files in the background. Every Trash or permanent deletion operation revalidates selected items and requires your explicit approval in Android's official system confirmation dialog (MediaStore.createTrashRequest / MediaStore.createDeleteRequest or RecoverableSecurityException on Android 10).

3. Optional App Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)

  • Explicit Prominent Disclosure: The optional Pro "Unused Apps" detector (highlighting user-installed apps not opened in 90+ days) is disabled by default and gated by an explicit in-app Prominent Disclosure modal before redirecting you to Android System Settings.
  • Strictly Local & Ephemeral: App usage timestamps queried via UsageStatsManager are evaluated exclusively in local device memory to display cleanup suggestions to you. Installed app lists and usage statistics are never collected, stored in the cloud, or shared with third parties.

4. Zero Advertising ID (AD_ID) & Zero Third-Party Ad Trackers

  • Midiastore contains no third-party advertising SDKs, does not request com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID, and performs zero cross-app tracking or behavioral profiling.
  • We never sell, rent, or share user data with data brokers or advertising networks.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

  • All Midiastore Pro plans — Monthly (sweep_pro_monthly), Annual (sweep_pro_annual), and One-Time Lifetime (sweep_pro_lifetime) — are billed and processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Midiastore never sees or stores credit card, bank, or billing address details.
  • Server-Side Entitlement & Fraud Verification: To verify Pro entitlements and handle renewals/refunds accurately, our HTTPS backend (Supabase Edge Functions) validates purchase state with the official Google Play Developer API v3 and records only minimal technical audit metadata: package_name, product_id, order_id, purchase/subscription status, expiration timestamp, and an irreversible SHA-256 cryptographic hash of the purchase_token (never the raw token). All network traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS.

6. Optional Closed-Test Waitlist & First-Party Diagnostics

  • Optional Web Opt-in: If you voluntarily submit your Google Play Gmail address on midiastory-df6a0.web.app, that email is stored solely to authorize your Google Play Closed Testing invitation and launch access.
  • Anonymous Aggregate Telemetry: To ensure app reliability and measure funnel health without invading privacy, our website and app log strictly anonymized first-party operational events (such as app version, locale, screen/funnel step, UTM campaign parameter, and a random non-identifying installation/visitor hash), never containing file names, media paths, personal identifiers, or device hardware IDs.

7. User Controls, Data Retention & Deletion Requests

  • On-Device Controls: You may revoke media or Usage Access permissions at any time in Android Settings, clear local app storage to erase all local scan history, and manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions.
  • Data Deletion & Privacy Inquiries: To request deletion of a voluntarily submitted closed-testing email address or ask any privacy question, contact the developer directly through the support email listed on the official Google Play Store page for Midiastore (com.rdf112.midiastore). Purchase audit hashes required for financial accounting and fraud prevention are retained strictly for statutory compliance and are never linked to your personal identity.
