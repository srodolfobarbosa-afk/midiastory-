## Descrição / Notas
The most dangerous decisions can happen when everyone is being rewarded for making them.

Steve Eisman explained something he learned before the 2008 financial crisis.

Banks were making enormous amounts of money issuing mortgages, even as lending standards deteriorated.

The rewards were immediate. The consequences came years later.

Steve Eisman has a phrase for this: “Incentives trump ethics every time."

It makes you question how often we judge decisions by their immediate results, without considering what behaviours we're rewarding.
