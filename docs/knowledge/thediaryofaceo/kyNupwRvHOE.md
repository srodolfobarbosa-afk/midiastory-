What if the hardest chapters of your life are preparing you for something greater?

As a child, Katt Williams loved reading about heroes who faced extraordinary challenges.

He noticed that before the happy ending, something always went wrong. Often, many things went wrong.

So when his own childhood became incredibly difficult, he held on to the belief that his story could still turn out differently.

At 13, he left home. Years later, he found his way into comedy.

Katt's life has taken some extraordinary turns, and in our conversation, he explains how the stories he read as a little boy influenced the way he experienced his own.
