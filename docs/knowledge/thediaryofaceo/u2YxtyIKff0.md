What happens when people stop believing that the future will be better than the past?

For generations, many people grew up expecting that if they worked hard, their children would have a better life.

But Senator Bernie Sanders raised an interesting question about what happens when that expectation begins to disappear.

He believes the growing pressure on working families helps explain why people are losing trust in political institutions.

And that when governments repeatedly promise change but fail to deliver, people begin questioning whether the system works for them at all.

It's a conversation that goes far beyond American politics.
