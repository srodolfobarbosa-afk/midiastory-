What if the decline of your cognitive function isn't a genetic inevitability, but a metabolic choice you’re making every single day? Board-certified neurologist Dr. David Perlmutter discusses cognitive decay and outlines the definitive protocol for lifelong brain health and dementia prevention.

Dr. David Perlmutter is a pioneering researcher who radically challenged the medical establishment by linking metabolic dysfunction to severe neurodegenerative diseases. He is the author of several groundbreaking texts, including his most recent release, _Brain Defenders._ This project aims to empower individuals to regain agency over their longevity by harnessing the brain's immune system to reverse and prevent cognitive decline.

*In this episode, he explains:*
*■ The Evil Twin Phenomenon:* How high blood sugar and inflammation hijack your brain's protective microglial immune cells and shift them into a destructive state that actively devours healthy synapses.
*■ The Power of Exercise:* Why building muscle helps secrete protective chemicals to actively regenerate neurons and block cognitive decay.
*■ The Oxytocin Shield:* How deep social connections and community integration fundamentally alter your brain's inflammatory response and keep your neural immune system in a supportive state.
*■ The Glymphatic Street Cleaners:* Why missing out on stage four deep sleep immediately cripples your brain's ability to sweep out toxic amyloid-beta plaques overnight.
*■ The 40Hz Reset:* How specific frequencies of flashing light and sound can revert rogue brain cells back into protective guardians and restore your baseline neural rhythms.
