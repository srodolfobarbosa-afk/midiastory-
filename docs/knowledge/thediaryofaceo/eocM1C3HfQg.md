## Descrição / Notas
Can America's democracy still be saved at the ballot box, or has big money already bought the outcome? Senator Bernie Sanders joins to lay out what it will take to save American democracy, working families and the future of the Democratic Party.

Senator Bernie Sanders is the longest-serving independent in U.S. congressional history and a two-time presidential candidate who ignited a modern political revolution. Through his bestselling book _It's OK to Be Angry About Capitalism_ and national tours against oligarchy, he leads the charge to restore power to the working class. His ongoing movement challenges entrenched political and corporate monopolies to prove that government must serve human needs over billionaire profits.

*In this episode, he explains:*
*■ Legalized Oligarchy:* Campaign finance rules and corporate lobbying have replaced democratic deliberation with a “pay to play” structure where elite donors quietly draft national policy behind closed doors.
*■ The Incrementalism Trap:* Demanding minor, safe policy compromises alienates struggling voters and drives disillusioned working class communities straight into the arms of authoritarian leaders.
*■ Unregulated Automation:* Handing unchecked artificial intelligence development over to private tech oligarchs will trigger catastrophic white collar and manual job losses without shared public oversight.
*■ Systemic Wealth Hoarding:* Imposing a targeted wealth tax and closing tax free borrowing loopholes on unrealized equity is essential to halt the multi trillion dollar wealth siphon bleeding working class households.
*■ Universal Basic Dignity:* Establishing nonnegotiable guarantees like Medicare for All permanently breaks the psychological chokehold corporate employers hold over an anxious, underinsured workforce.
